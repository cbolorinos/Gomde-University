The Challenge

Europe has extraordinary knowledge, institutions, and technology. But there is a growing gap between what we know and how we live.

Our leaders face unprecedented complexity, yet qualities such as wisdom, discernment, compassion, courage, and responsibility are rarely cultivated as seriously as technical skills.

Where do we develop the human qualities needed to meet the challenges of our time?

The Opportunity

We want to help fill that gap by creating a place where consciousness and competence develop together.

A place where modern science meets contemplative wisdom; where different traditions can engage in genuine dialogue; and where leaders, educators, scientists, entrepreneurs, and young people can explore how to live and lead with greater clarity and purpose.

Why Austria?

At Schloss Kremsegg, Chökyi Nyima Rinpoche is developing a vision for a university, educational center, and contemplative place of learning — rooted in the Buddhist tradition and open to all.

Austria offers a central European setting for something larger: a European meeting place for wisdom, education, dialogue, and human development.

Not simply another conference center or retreat — but a place where people can step back, learn, reflect, and ask:

What kind of human beings do we need to become to create the future we want?

Why You?

This project needs people from across European society.

You don't need to be a Buddhist.
You don't need to be an academic.

You can contribute through your experience, ideas, networks, resources, or simply your willingness to explore what is possible.
